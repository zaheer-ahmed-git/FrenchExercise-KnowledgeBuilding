# French Script Style (shared by both workflows)

The learner is currently **A2** and progressing toward **B1–B2**. Scripts must be challenging but followable by ear.

## Length and structure

- **900–1200 words** (hard limits 750–1500), about 6–10 minutes of audio.
- 8–14 paragraphs separated by blank lines.
- Suggested flow: hook and context, what happened, how it works (simple explanation), why it matters, limits or open questions, short recap.

## Language level

- Sentences mostly 12–20 words; occasional longer sentence at B2.
- Tenses: présent, passé composé, imparfait, futur proche, futur simple; some conditionnel and subjonctif at B2.
- Use connectors that structure listening: *d'abord, ensuite, en effet, cependant, pourtant, par conséquent, autrement dit, en résumé*.
- Explain each technical term the first time in plain French ("un modèle de langage, c'est-à-dire un programme qui...").
- Keep established English tech terms when French speakers use them (*open source*, *benchmark*, *GPU*), but introduce the French equivalent when one is common (*apprentissage automatique*, *jeu de données*).
- Numbers: write them as words when short ("trois milliards") so the TTS reads them naturally; avoid long digit strings, tables, URLs, code, and emojis in the script.
- Natural spoken French: rhetorical questions and signposting ("Alors, pourquoi est-ce important ?").

## Accuracy

- Facts come only from `_research.md` notes. Do not add details that the sources do not support.
- Attribute claims ("selon l'équipe de Hugging Face...").
